# ShopMind Electronics: Return, Replacement & Refund Policy

This is a fictional policy created for the ShopMind portfolio demo.
It does not represent any real retailer's policy.

## 1. Return windows

| Category | Window (from delivery date) | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Smartphones | 7 days | **Replacement only** (same model), for defects or damage |
| Laptops & tablets | 7 days | Replacement only, after a technician check |
| Earbuds & headphones | 7 days | Replacement only. **Not returnable if the seal is opened** (hygiene), unless defective |
| Accessories (chargers, cables, cases) | 10 days | Return for refund or replacement |
| Smartwatches & wearables | 7 days | Replacement only, for defects |

## 2. Damaged, defective or wrong item

- If the item arrives **physically damaged** (e.g. cracked screen, broken box) or is the **wrong item**, report it **within 48 hours of delivery**.
- Share **clear photos** of the item, the box and the shipping label when you raise the request.
- A **defective** item (does not switch on, faulty display, charging issue) is eligible within the window above after a quick check by our team or the brand's service centre.

## 3. Not eligible for return or replacement

- Damage caused by the customer: drops, cracks after use, **liquid or water damage**, burn marks.
- Items with a **missing IMEI/serial number**, tampered seals, or missing original box, accessories, manuals or freebies.
- Software issues that can be fixed by an update or reset.
- **Change of mind** for smartphones, laptops and opened earbuds.
- Requests made **after** the return window.

## 4. Condition for returns

- The item must be in **original condition** with the box, all accessories, manuals, warranty card and invoice.
- For phones and laptops, **remove all accounts and screen locks** (Google/Apple ID, PIN) and back up your data before pickup.

## 5. How to request a return

1. Go to **My Orders**, choose the order and select **Return / Replace**.
2. Pick the reason and upload photos if the item is damaged or wrong.
3. Our team reviews the request within **24–48 hours**.
4. If approved, a **pickup** is scheduled. A replacement ships after the item passes the quality check at pickup.

## 6. Refunds

- Refunds apply only where a return (not replacement) is allowed, or when a replacement is out of stock.
- Refunds go to the **original payment method** within **5–7 working days** after the item passes the quality check.
- **Cash on Delivery** orders are refunded to your bank account or UPI ID.
- Shipping charges are refunded only if the item was damaged, defective or wrong.

## 7. Warranty

After the return window, all products are covered by the **brand's manufacturer warranty** (usually 1 year for phones and laptops, 6 months to 1 year for accessories). Warranty claims are handled at the brand's authorised service centre.

## 8. Final decision

All return and refund requests are **verified by our support team**. The AI assistant can explain this policy and help you start a request, but it **cannot approve refunds by itself**.
